Privacy Policy

AI Love Advice: Dating Coach
The short version: AI Love Advice has no accounts and no sign-up. Your chats, your mini-profile and any screenshots you attach stay on your device. We do not run a server that stores them. The only thing that leaves your phone is the message you send for advice, which goes to OpenAI so an answer can be generated. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains how Swifty Solutions Company Limited ("Swifty Solutions", "we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the AI Love Advice mobile application, also listed as "AI Love Advice: Dating Coach" (the "App"), available on iOS and Android.

Effective date: September 16, 2026

1. No Account, No Identity Data

The App does not have accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, no email verification and no social sign-in. You can install the App and use it immediately.

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) and we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission for advertising purposes.
  • We do not build a user profile, and we do not link any data to your identity.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is created by you while using the App and is stored locally on your device only. It is not uploaded to, or retained by, any server operated by Swifty Solutions.

Stored locally on your device:

  • Chat messages: the questions you type and the AI answers you receive, organised into conversations.
  • Optional mini-profile: a nickname, gender, age range, relationship status, the first name of the person you are discussing, and your goal. Every field is optional and is used only to give the AI context for better advice.
  • Attached screenshots: images of conversations you choose to attach to a message.
  • App preferences: settings such as language and appearance.

We do not operate a database, backend account system or cloud backup for this content. If you enable your own device backup (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup), your operating system may include App data in that backup under your own account and under the terms of Apple or Google. That backup belongs to you, not to us.

3. What Is Transmitted to Generate an Answer

The App uses a third-party AI model to produce advice. When you send a message, the following is transmitted to OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States) through the OpenAI API:

  • The content of your current message.
  • A trimmed portion of the recent conversation (up to approximately the last 12 turns) so the answer stays in context.
  • Up to 2 attached images included with that message, if you attached any.
  • The optional mini-profile details you have filled in, when they are relevant context for the request.

Nothing else is sent. Older conversations, other chats and images you did not attach are not transmitted. The transmission is used only to generate the reply that is shown back to you.

OpenAI's handling of this content is governed by its own privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. As stated by OpenAI, data submitted through the OpenAI API is not used to train its models by default.

Please keep this in mind: anything you type or attach is sent to the AI provider in order to be answered. Avoid including information you would not want processed by a third party, such as passwords, financial details, identity documents or the private details of other people.

4. No Analytics, No Advertising, No Tracking

  • The App contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK that profiles you.
  • The App shows no advertising and contains no ad networks.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Because there is no account and no analytics, we have no way to identify you, contact you, or reconstruct what you discussed in the App.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions and any in-app purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment is made to the store, not to us.

  • We never see or receive your card number, bank details or billing address.
  • The App only receives, from the store, an anonymous indication of whether an active subscription exists.
  • Store purchases are subject to Apple's and Google's own privacy policies and terms.

6. Photos and Media

The App requests access to your photo library only at the moment you choose to attach a screenshot. We access only the image you pick, and only for the purposes of the current message. We do not scan your library, index your photos, or read images you did not select. The selected image stays on your device (attached to that chat) and is sent to the AI provider only as part of that request.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • Your chats, mini-profile and attachments remain on your device until you remove them.
  • You can delete an individual chat at any time, or use "Clear all chats" in the App to remove all of them at once.
  • Uninstalling the App removes the App's local data from your device.
  • Deletion is immediate and permanent. Because we hold no copy on a server, deleted content cannot be recovered by us or by you.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. It is not intended for anyone under 13 years of age, or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. The App is rated for mature audiences and is intended for adults seeking dating and relationship advice. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, deleting the App's chats and removing the App will remove the local data from the device.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA) or similar laws, including rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection.

Because the App is designed so that your content stays on your device and we hold no copy, you exercise these rights directly through the App's own controls:

  • Access: open the App - your chats and mini-profile are visible and fully under your control.
  • Correction: edit or clear your mini-profile fields at any time.
  • Deletion: delete a chat, use "Clear all chats", or uninstall the App.
  • Portability and objection: since no personal data is held by us, there is nothing for us to export or process on your behalf.

We have no means of identifying you and therefore cannot look up, export or delete data on your behalf. If you have questions about these rights, contact us at contact@swifty-solutions.com.

Under the CCPA/CPRA we confirm that we do not sell and do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. International Transfers

OpenAI, the provider that generates the App's answers, operates in the United States. If you use the App from outside the United States, the message content described in Section 3 is transmitted to and processed in the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence. This transfer happens solely to fulfil your request for an answer, and takes place only when you send a message.

11. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

⚠ Important: The advice in this App is produced by an AI language model. It may be inaccurate, incomplete, generic or simply wrong. It is not therapy, counselling, medical advice, legal advice, or a crisis or emergency service, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional. Always apply your own judgement before acting on what the App says.

If you are in danger, being abused, or thinking about harming yourself or someone else, please stop using the App and contact your local emergency services immediately, or reach a trained human at a helpline. You can find a helpline in your country at findahelpline.com.

12. Security

  • Your chats, mini-profile and attachments are stored in the App's private storage area and are protected by your device's own protections - passcode, biometric lock and operating-system file encryption.
  • All communication with the AI provider is encrypted in transit using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Because your content lives on your device, keeping your device locked and up to date is the most effective protection.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. A lost, shared, unlocked or compromised device may expose the App's local content to others.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App gains new features or changes providers. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top and bottom of this page and, where the change is material, provide notice in the App. Your continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
