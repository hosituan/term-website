Privacy Policy

TikCar

Your Privacy at a Glance

TikCar does not run its own servers and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to TikCar. Your TikTok session, cached feed, and app preferences live only on your device.

Information We Process

TikCar is a client app that lets you browse the TikTok "For You" feed in a more focused way and stream the audio of those videos through CarPlay. The following information is processed locally on your device:

  • TikTok session cookies — set by TikTok inside the in-app sign-in WebView. These remain on your device and are sent back to TikTok only when you load or refresh the feed.
  • Cached video metadata — titles, captions, author handles, and CDN URLs for the most recent ~60 items, stored in the app's Caches folder so a cold CarPlay launch can show your last-known feed.
  • App preferences — playback speed, auto-scroll toggle, selected language, and premium entitlement state, stored in iOS UserDefaults.

None of this information is uploaded to TikCar. We have no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no server-side logging.

Information You Send to TikTok

When you sign in to TikTok inside the app's sign-in WebView, you are interacting directly with TikTok. Anything you type (phone, email, password, verification codes) is handled by TikTok's web pages and governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy. TikCar does not see, read, or record those credentials.

When you browse the feed, TikCar fetches video lists from TikTok's web endpoints on your behalf. These requests include your TikTok session cookies and standard browser metadata (user agent, locale, time zone) as required by TikTok.

Permissions We Request

  • Photos (write only) — requested only when you tap "Save to Photos" in the long-press action sheet. Used to add the downloaded video to your library. We do not read or browse your photo library.
  • CarPlay (audio) — required to display the feed as a CarPlay list and play audio in the car. No data is sent off-device through this permission.

In-App Purchases

TikCar offers optional Premium plans (Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime) processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never receive your payment information. Premium entitlement is verified through Apple's signed receipts on your device.

Data Retention & Deletion

Everything TikCar stores is local. To delete it:

  • Tap Sign out in Settings to clear TikTok cookies and the cached feed.
  • Delete the TikCar app to remove all local data, including preferences and the entitlement cache.

Children's Privacy

TikCar is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to retain or disclose under COPPA. Please review TikTok's own age requirements before signing in.

Third Parties

TikCar communicates with TikTok's servers (to load the feed) and Apple's servers (StoreKit, CarPlay). It does not contact any other third party. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
